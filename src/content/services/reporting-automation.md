### What's included

- A review of your recurring reports: where the data comes from, who touches it and how long it takes
- An automated pipeline from source systems to finished report, with data quality checks built in
- Scheduled delivery by email, Teams, Google Chat or a shared drive
- Handover documentation and a walkthrough for the people who own the process

### How it runs

A fixed-scope project, one reporting process at a time. You get a written scope and timeline before any work starts.
