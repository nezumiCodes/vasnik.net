### What's included

- A KPI session with the people who will use the dashboard, so it answers their real questions
- Data cleaning and modelling from your existing sources: databases, SharePoint, Google Sheets or Microsoft Forms
- Dashboards built around decisions, with drill-downs where they help
- Automated refreshes and access controls
- Training for dashboard users and owners

### How it runs

A fixed-scope project per dashboard or reporting area, with a working first version early so your team can shape the final one.

### Track record (employed roles)

At QA, Power BI and Tableau dashboards gave `30+` stakeholders self-service access to KPIs and replaced `20+` manual weekly reports. At We Are With You, Power BI dashboards are used by `60+` managers across `8` services.
